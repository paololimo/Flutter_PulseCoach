Direction 1 Analysis

Strengths
  • All 3 sessions visible with light scroll — clear daily overview
  • Each card is self-contained: type, meta, explanation, Start
  • Explanation always visible (no tap to expand)
  • Completion ring at bottom tracks daily progress
  • Most faithful to PRD specification
  • Simple to implement with standard Flutter Column + Card
Considerations
  • All cards compete equally for attention — no clear hierarchy of "do this next"
  • Three Start buttons visible simultaneously may feel like choices, not a queue
  • Scroll required to see 3rd card + completion ring

Direction 2 Analysis

Strengths
  • Clear hierarchy: one primary action, no decision paralysis
  • Hero card matches "Spotify autoplay" mental model — the next track is obvious
  • Upcoming sessions visible but recessive — available, not competing
  • AI explanation prominent on hero card only
  • Gradient background adds premium feel without being loud
  • Everything above the fold — no scroll needed for primary action
Considerations
  • Upcoming sessions lose their AI explanations (only meta visible)
  • Tapping an upcoming session needs to expand or navigate — extra step
  • Gradient background may feel inconsistent with the "dark canvas" principle

Direction 3 Analysis

Strengths
  • Time-of-day anchors make the plan feel scheduled and intentional
  • Visual timeline communicates "daily flow" — morning→afternoon→evening
  • Next session highlighted with glowing dot — clear visual hierarchy
  • Personal greeting adds warmth
  • AI explanations visible on all cards
Considerations
  • Time anchors imply scheduling — conflicts with "do it whenever you have a gap" flexibility
  • Users may feel they "missed" the 08:00 session if they open at 10:00
  • Timeline metaphor suggests obligation rather than opportunity
  • Start button only on the "next" session — may feel restrictive

Direction 4 Analysis

Strengths
  • All 3 sessions + stats visible without scrolling
  • Stats row gives immediate context (RPE, steps, session count)
  • Compact cards are scannable and efficient
  • Arrow buttons are clear action targets
  • Everything fits above the fold
Considerations
  • Stats row contradicts "warmth over metrics" principle — numbers first
  • Compact cards truncate AI explanations (ellipsis)
  • Dense layout conflicts with "airy" spacing philosophy
  • Feels more like a dashboard than a wellness companion
  • Better suited for tablet layout than phone primary

Direction 5 Analysis

Strengths
  • Ring as hero element — Oura-inspired, visually distinctive
  • Progress is the first thing the user sees — motivating without shaming
  • State banner is prominent and well-contextualized
  • Featured first card with subtle border = clear "do this next"
  • AI explanations visible on all session cards
Considerations

  • Ring is a progress metric — may conflict with "warmth over metrics"
  • "0/3" on first open could feel empty or daunting (3 sessions to do!)
  • The ring is the hero but the session is the action — attention mismatch

Direction 6 Analysis

Strengths
  • Maximum focus: one session at a time, no competition
  • Swipe gesture is natural and engaging
  • Each card has full space for explanation + Start button
  • Premium feel — immersive, no visual clutter
  • Dot indicators show position in queue
Considerations
  • No daily overview — user can't see all 3 sessions at a glance
  • Swipe interaction adds a step vs. vertical scroll
  • Completion ring not visible — no daily progress indicator
  • Horizontal scroll is less standard in Flutter/mobile wellness apps
  • May conflict with "daily plan as a coherent set" principle

Core Flow Notes

  • In-Session: Full-screen, no navigation. Timer (JetBrains Mono 56sp), current step, instruction, progress bar, HR top-right. "End session" small and recessive at bottom.
  • RPE: Single question. 10 circular buttons. One tap = submission. No confirmation dialog. Scale labels at extremes only. Subtitle reminds user their feedback matters.
  • Summary: Auto-dismisses after 3 seconds. Shows completion check, duration, RPE, avg HR, one-line system feedback. Fades back to Today screen.
  • Countdown (not shown): Before session starts, 3-2-1 countdown with JetBrains Mono 72sp numbers, soft fade animation, ~2 seconds total.
