## What Went Well

1. **Linear, blocker-free execution (Project Lead's read).** Five stories shipped in clean test-count increments, `flutter analyze` at zero throughout, no architectural surprises, no schema migration. A calm finale.
2. **Both MVP-if-time stories delivered.** 14.4 (AI decision log) and 14.5 (data export) were explicitly descopable ("if time permits"). Both landed — the team reached the v1 line with margin, not on fumes.
3. **Clean dependency isolation.** The three new packages (`shared_preferences`, `share_plus`, `path_provider`) touched no domain or AI layer. `ThemeCubit` moved from binary toggle to 3-mode + persistence without ripples; `app.dart` wiring needed no change.
4. **Strong follow-through on E13R-3.** The Epic 13 retro action — "verify bandit decision-history persistence before writing Story 14.4 Tasks" — paid off: 14.4 is a **read-only projection repository** that reconstructs the decision log from RPE feedback + session_logs + daily_plans (no dedicated table), with an `unknown` fallback for corrupt/missing plan context (`14.4-REPO-002`). The forecast was honored and prevented a wrong assumption.
5. **On-device verification confirmed the units couldn't reach.** The 3 PARTIAL P2 ACs that worried QA (14.5-AC3/AC4 share sheet + file export, via the `share_plus` platform channel) all passed on device — the OS share sheet ("Completa azione usando") appearing is the end-to-end proof the matrix's PARTIAL flag could not give. The known `Strengt`/`h` segmented-control overflow did **not** recur on the two new `SegmentedButton`s.

## Challenges & Carry-Forward Notes

1. **Recurring "verification done but record slips."** Epic 14 was fully verified on the SM-A520F — but the report was not initially in `CLAUDE.md`'s Test/Build baseline. Same failure mode flagged at Epic 11 and Epic 13 (E13R-1). **Closed this retro** by recording the full report (E14R-1). The lesson holds: on a quiet, linear epic the *record* slips precisely because nothing breaks and nobody goes to look.
2. **~~Asymmetric code-review record~~ (E14R-2 — FALSE ALARM, retracted 2026-06-05).** The retro initially flagged that 14.2–14.5 appeared to lack `Review Findings` sections. This was a **facilitation error**: the analysis grep matched `^## Review Findings` (level-2) while the actual headers are `### Review Findings` (level-3, nested under Dev Agent Record). On re-check, **all five stories carry substantive 3-layer adversarial reviews dated 2026-06-05** that caught real bugs: 14.2 (2 patches — AppBar test scoping, body-text assertion), 14.3 (4 patches — duplicate WearOS label, ICU plural, a missing `await` that defeated the try/catch, dead `copyWith`), 14.4 (1 decision + 3 patches + 1 defer — dead-scaffolding removal, try/catch against infinite spinner on DB error, `Equatable`, sort tiebreaker), 14.5 (3 patches + 1 defer — emit-after-close race, temp-file cleanup, AC5/AC6 coverage). No record gap exists. **Lesson for the facilitator:** verify heading-level assumptions before asserting a gap — the same "forecast/assert without checking current state" failure mode E13R-2 warns about, this time applied to the retro analysis itself.
3. **4 PARTIAL P2 ACs are device-only by nature.** 14.4-AC2 (`ExpansionTile`, no custom logic), 14.4-AC4 (`kDebugMode` compile-time constant), 14.5-AC3/AC4 (`share_plus` platform channel). Not unit-testable without on-device integration tests — the gate accepted them with justification and on-device verification covered the observable behavior. No action beyond awareness.

**Theme:** As with Epic 13, the failure modes were not in engineering but in **record discipline** — verification and review work was done; consolidating the evidence into the durable record was the lagging step. Closed for verification (E14R-1); review-record consistency tracked as E14R-2.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Quiet epics need *louder* record discipline.** Nothing broke in Epic 14, so the verification record nearly slipped again — the third time this pattern surfaced (11 → 13 → 14). Closing E14R-1 in-retro is the right reflex; the durable fix is making the record step part of "done," not an afterthought.
2. **Forecast-then-verify works.** E13R-3 turned a potentially wrong assumption (a persisted bandit-decision table) into a correct read-only projection design for 14.4. Carrying a *specific, checkable* forecast into the next create-story is high-leverage.
3. **MVP-if-time delivered = healthy margin.** Shipping both descopable stories at the v1 finish line is a signal the roadmap was paced well.
