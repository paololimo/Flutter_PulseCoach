## What Went Well

### 1. E8-P1 (Cubit-lifecycle AC set) paid off as a preventive control
All three new cubits shipped with idempotency guards, `isClosed`/`close()` cancellation, and explicit `error(Failure)` states **by design**, with at least one test per invariant. The pattern that was painfully re-discovered three times in Epic 8 (8.2/8.3/8.4 `start()` non-idempotency) was specified up front. No `start()`/trigger non-idempotency findings surfaced at Epic 9 review. The AC template moved the fix from review-time to spec-time.

### 2. E7.5-T1 finally closed after slipping across three epics
The 7 dead ARB `transition*` keys (open since Epic 7.5, re-targeted 7.5 → 8 → 9) were wired in Story 9.3 via a `BehavioralTransitionKey` enum propagated end-to-end (`BehavioralStateMachine` → `AiEngineOutput` → `DailyPlanBloc` → `StateIndicator`). The stretch was absorbed without expanding epic scope, and it freed a Category A deliverable-debt slot.

### 3. E8-P3 (schema-bump cadence cap) respected on first application
A single v7→v8 bump for `rpe_feedback.sessionLogId`, with an explicit justification in the Story 9.1 spec that no other Epic 9 change could be consolidated with it. None of the v4→v7-in-48-hours churn that triggered the rule in Epic 8.

### 4. Multi-reviewer apparatus caught a real BLOCKER in 9.1
9.1-P1: `RpeFeedbackCompanion(sessionId: _args?.planId ?? 0)` wrote the *plan* id into a column documented as a logical FK to `sessions.id`, and `0` on null args — an orphan indistinguishable from a real session that Story 9.3's reward path would have read as garbage. No test covered it; the Acceptance Auditor did. Patched pre-merge with a `SessionLogsDao` lookup.

## What Did Not Go Well

### 1. RPE overflow escaped the test suite and shipped a layout FAIL caught only on device
Widget test `9.1-WIDGET-004` verified the ≥44dp target size, but the default `flutter_test` surface is **800dp wide**. The single horizontal row of 10 targets overflowed by **204dp** on the Samsung A520F at 360dp, clipping RPE 8/9/10 off-screen. It was discovered manually **after** Story 9.1 closed, on a separate branch (`fix/rpe-input-overflow-360dp`, commit `979acb5`). The fix degrades to two rows of five below ~516dp — a **documented deviation from UX-DR9** ("single horizontal row") still pending UX/PM validation.

**Root cause:** widget tests pass at 800dp and lie about phone-width behavior. This is exactly the gap E7-T2 (golden/viewport test infra) was meant to close — still `pending`, coupled to Epic 11.

**Decision (Paolo, in retro):** (a) validate the 2-row deviation now via a UX/PM mini-review; (b) unblock E7-T2 viewport-test infra before Story 10.2.

### 2. E8-T1 (centralized logger) was triggered and not closed
The E8-T1 trigger was explicit: *"first Epic 9.x story that touches `lib/core/error/` or introduces a new persistence error path"* — i.e. Story 9.1. The stories did the right thing at the AC level (explicit error state, no `debugPrint`-only), which mitigated the worst Epic 8 symptom (the user being "lied to" on silent failure). But the **deliverable** — centralizing the logger — was not done; Story 9.3 still uses scattered `developer.log`. Same "triggered and ignored" anti-pattern that bit E7.5-P2 in Epic 8. The item sits in Category A occupying a slot.

**Decision (Paolo, in retro):** promote E8-T1 to a dedicated scheduled story in Epic 10.

### 3. On-device smoke absent for two consecutive epics
`adb devices` returned empty during all three Epic 9 stories. Combined with the still-open Epic 8 action item #7 (Story 8.5 abandon-flow on-device smoke, never executed), the CLAUDE.md "On-Device UI Verification Protocol" has now been effectively un-enforced across two epics. The RPE overflow is the direct cost: a layout FAIL only the physical device revealed.

**Decision (Paolo, in retro):** unblock E7-T2 viewport-test infra (CI coverage at 360dp, device-independent) before Story 10.2.

## Key Insights

1. **Lifecycle invariants as an AC template convert review-time bugs into spec-time guarantees.** E8-P1's first full epic showed the payoff: zero cubit-lifecycle findings at review across three new cubits.
2. **Widget tests at the default 800dp surface systematically miss phone-width layout failures.** The RPE overflow is the proof. Viewport/golden tests at 360dp (E7-T2) are no longer "nice to have" — they are the only CI-side defense for layout, and Epic 10's four charts are the next exposure.
3. **"Triggered and ignored" is not exclusive to process rules — it hit a Category A deliverable (E8-T1) this epic.** The fire-check (E9-K1) must extend to deliverable-debt items with relative triggers, surfaced at story-creation.
4. **Absorbing a long-slipping stretch (E7.5-T1) is feasible when the consumer signature is already being touched** — confirming the Epic 8 retro re-target heuristic was correct.
5. **On-device verification cannot depend on ad-hoc ADB availability.** Two epics with no device smoke means the protocol needs a device-independent substitute (viewport tests) plus a planned device gate, not a hope that the phone is connected.
6. **Single, justified schema bumps keep migration churn and dev-env inconsistency low** — E8-P3 working as intended.
