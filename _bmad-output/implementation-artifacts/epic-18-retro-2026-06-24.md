## 3. Challenges & Recurring Patterns

### 3.1 "Small-device overflow" — now 4 epics running (root cause to be closed)
On-device gate caught what desk review did not, again:
- Epic 9 (RPEInput @360dp) → Epic 16 (SignInSheet + keyboard) → Epic 17 (paywall RC string) → **Epic 18 (`_FriendsShimmer` BOTTOM OVERFLOWED BY 54 PIXELS)**.
- Root cause this time: `_FriendsShimmer` used a non-scrollable fixed-height `Column` (header 56 + 5×72 ≈ 452 logical px) inside a nested `Scaffold`; overflows on the SM-A520F's shorter logical height. The *loaded* state (`_FriendsList`) already used a scrollable `ListView`; the shimmer was inconsistent. Not caught by the 800px-tall default `flutter_test` surface.
- **Fixed in-session** (`Column` → `ListView`), re-verified PASS. Paolo's directive: close the root cause structurally (see E18R-1).

### 3.2 E17R-1 recurred — inconsistent error localization across social tabs
The Epic 17 action "map backend-failure paths to localized IT messages" was applied only partially in Epic 18: **Feed and Confronto** show the localized "Qualcosa è andato storto. Riprova.", but the **Amici** tab surfaces the raw English exception `Failed to fetch friends: Bad state: No authenticated session` (`failure.message` shown verbatim when non-empty). A Pro-but-not-signed-in user hits the raw English string on Amici in production. Pro and auth-session are two distinct gates.

### 3.3 Minor
- `ProUpsellSheet` body copy is the generic Epic-17 "storico e grafici" fact even when entered from the Social locked banner (no social-specific copy).
- E10R-2 extended: Story 18.4's `GetOwnWeeklySummaryUseCase._thisWeekMinutes` repeats the UTC Monday-label computation; no non-UTC regression test was added.
